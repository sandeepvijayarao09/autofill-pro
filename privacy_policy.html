AutoFill Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  ·  Effective immediately upon installation

Short version: Without an NVIDIA API key, nothing leaves your device. If you add a key and AI is on, the AI features send form questions, parts of your profile and (for resume import) your resume text to NVIDIA NIM. Your email, phone and street address are removed from resume text before it is sent. Date of birth, EEO data (race/ethnicity, gender, pronouns, disability, veteran status), phone, salary, visa status and security clearance are never sent from your profile.

1. What data we collect and store

AutoFill Pro stores the following data exclusively on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API:

  • Profile information you enter in the extension popup (name, email, address, work history, education, etc.)
  • Your NVIDIA API key (if you choose to enable AI features)
  • Extension settings (AI on/off, floating button on/off, AI model preference)
  • Button position on screen (stored in sessionStorage, cleared when you close the browser tab)

We do not operate any server. No data is synced to any cloud service controlled by us. The extension has no backend.

2. What data leaves your device

Pattern-based filling runs entirely in your browser. Data is sent to an external service only when you have saved an NVIDIA API key in Settings and "AI matching" is on (it is on by default, but does nothing without a key). Requests go to NVIDIA's API (integrate.api.nvidia.com) and nowhere else. There are four cases:

  • Fields the patterns cannot match. The field labels, placeholders and ids, plus your profile minus the fields in section 3, so that the AI can pick a value. This includes your name, email, address, work history, education, links and summary if you have filled them in.
  • Custom questions ("Why do you want to work here?"). The question text plus the same filtered profile.
  • Answers longer than a field's character limit. The text being filled, so it can be shortened to fit.
  • Resume import (Import & Fill Profile). Up to 5,000 characters of the text you pasted or that was read from your PDF. Before sending, the extension removes email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses and US city/state/ZIP lines on your device and fills those fields itself. Everything else in the resume (for example your name, employers, education and links) is sent as written. PDFs are read locally; the file itself is never uploaded.

3. What is never sent from your profile

These profile fields are removed from every AI request built from your profile:

  • Date of birth
  • Race / Ethnicity
  • Disability status
  • Veteran status
  • Gender / Pronouns
  • Security clearance level
  • Phone number
  • Expected salary
  • Visa / work authorization status

Resume import works on free text, not on these fields, so if your resume itself mentions one of them (other than the contact details removed above), that text is sent along with the rest.

4. Third-party services

When AI is enabled, form field data is processed by NVIDIA NIM API. Their data handling is governed by NVIDIA's Privacy Policy. AutoFill Pro does not share data with any other third party.

5. Data retention

Your profile data is stored locally until you clear it in the extension popup or uninstall the extension. We have no ability to access or delete data on your behalf.

6. Permissions used

  • storage — stores your profile and settings locally on your device
  • activeTab — reads and fills the currently active browser tab's form fields
  • webNavigation — detects iframes on pages like Workday and Taleo so their embedded forms can also be filled
  • <all_urls> — required because job applications exist across thousands of different websites; the extension must be able to run on any domain

7. Children's privacy

AutoFill Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the extension's support page or contact the developer directly through the Chrome Web Store listing.
